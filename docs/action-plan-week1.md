## 📅 Day 6-7: Content Planning

### 🎯 Learning Goals:
- Plan your first 10 stories for KATHA
- Research Indian heritage topics
- Create content structure

### 📚 Research Tasks:
1. **Story Categories**
   - Mythology: Ramayana, Mahabharata stories
   - History: Mauryan Empire, Mughal Era
   - Folk Tales: Birbal stories, regional tales
   - Geography: Sacred rivers, mountain ranges
   - Architecture: Temple styles, fort designs

2. **Content Structure Planning**
   - Each story needs: Title, summary, full content, images, category
   - Think about difficulty levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced
   - Plan interactive elements: quizzes, timelines, maps

3. **Write Your First Story**
   - Choose one simple folk tale (like Akbar-Birbal)
   - Write it in 300-500 words
   - Structure: Introduction, main story, moral/lesson, historical context
